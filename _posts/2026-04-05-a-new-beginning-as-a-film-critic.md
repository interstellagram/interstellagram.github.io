I have been watching films since childhood, first drawn in by the pure joy and excitement of the experience itself. Over time, that simple enjoyment grew into a deeper interest in how films tell stories, create emotion, and leave a lasting impression.

Among all the directors I have watched, Christopher Nolan has had one of the strongest impacts on me. His films were genuinely shocking to me in the best way, not only because of their scale and originality, but also because of the ideas and emotions behind them. For that reason, I want to begin my first series of film review columns with Christopher Nolan's work.

I have been considering starting on a GitHub blog, but I feel that it may be less accessible to general audiences. Since I want to connect with more readers and build an audience from the beginning, I am also thinking about starting on another social media platform instead.

I am still new to social media, so I have even been thinking carefully about what to write in my profile and what kind of account name I should use. At the moment, I think I will choose a name that combines the word "SHA" with the title of one of Christopher Nolan's films. I like the idea of making my account name feel personal while also showing the kind of films and thoughts that inspired this new beginning.

This may be a small first step, but I hope it becomes the start of something meaningful. From now on, I want to share my thoughts on films more seriously, connect with other people who love cinema, and continue growing as a writer and reviewer. If you happen to come across this post, I would truly appreciate your support. Please follow me and join me as I begin this new journey.
