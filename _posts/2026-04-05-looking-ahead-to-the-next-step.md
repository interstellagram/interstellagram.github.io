As I continue this new journey of writing about films, I keep thinking about what kind of space would suit these reviews best. I want a place where thoughts can be shared more freely, where short impressions can still leave a strong impact, and where conversations can spread quickly in just a few words. After looking into it a bit more, I realized that there is a 280-character limit, which makes me a little concerned.

Lately, I have been thinking again about the film that means the most to me. Out of all the films I have watched, it is the one that stayed with me the longest. Its scale, emotion, and sense of hope still feel unforgettable.

"We'll find a way. We always have."

That line feels especially meaningful to me now. Maybe it is because every new beginning starts with uncertainty, but also with the belief that there is still a way forward.

Perhaps my next step will be on a different platform. A place for shorter thoughts, faster exchanges, and a new kind of beginning.

While watching films, I found it quite fun to write things in Morse code like a secret message now and then.
